# Operational & Legal Limitations

## 1. Non-Equivalence Axiom
- **AI Output is NOT Legal Evidence**: Machine learning scores and anomaly rankings indicate analytical triage priority only. They cannot replace statutory chain-of-custody protocols or forensic expert testimony.
- **Cryptographic Hashing != Acquisition Validity**: A valid SHA-256 hash confirms only that an evidence record has not changed since acquisition; it does not validate that the original data source was authentic or legally obtained.
- **Immutability != Legal Admissibility**: Anchoring a digest to a blockchain establishes temporal existence and immutability, but does not independently establish admissibility under jurisdiction-specific procedural rules (e.g. Federal Rules of Evidence 901/902 in the US, PACE in the UK).

---

## 2. Adversarial Evasion & Peeling
- Sophisticated adversaries utilizing automated peeling chains or decentralized cross-chain bridges may deliberately introduce delays to bypass 1-hour and 24-hour temporal velocity windows.
- Privacy-enhanced cryptocurrencies (e.g. Monero RingCT / Zcash zk-SNARKs) require zero-knowledge cryptographic decoding hooks not present on transparent UTXO/account ledgers.
