# Future Research Extensions & Roadmap

## 1. Dynamic Temporal Graph Neural Networks (TGNNs)
- Implementing continuous-time dynamic graph neural networks (e.g. TGAT, TGN) with memory modules to learn temporal wallet embeddings over streaming mempools.
- Evaluating memory overhead and inference latency tradeoffs compared to the baseline Random Forest.

---

## 2. Multi-Chain Cross-Bridge Graph Stitching
- Extending the incremental temporal graph engine to stitch UTXO-based chains (Bitcoin, Litecoin) with Account-based EVM chains (Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon) and Solana across known cross-chain liquidity pool bridge contracts.

---

## 3. Zero-Knowledge Evidence Proofs (zk-SNARKs)
- Generating zk-SNARK proofs of evidence integrity, allowing law enforcement agencies to cryptographically prove possession of authentic forensic evidence without disclosing sensitive counterparty addresses before court warrants are executed.
